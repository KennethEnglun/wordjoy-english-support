START HERE

Getting started

Start with over 1,000 picture vocabulary cards, then practise through games. The interface and meanings use Traditional Chinese; the subject is English.

  1. 01

    Look and listen

    Open 詞語寶庫 (Vocabulary) from Home. Choose a level and topic to see pictures and hear English words and examples.

  2. 02

    Make a practice pack

    Open 我的練習包 (My practice packs), create a pack and add built-in cards. Creating a pack is free.

  3. 03

    Choose a game

    Tap 開始冒險 (Start adventure), choose a game, topic or pack, then select the difficulty and mode.

  4. 04

    Review progress

    Use 報告 (Reports) to see game history and common mistakes. Collect stickers with coins earned in games.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Common questions

Do practice packs require payment?

No. Create packs with built-in or existing cards for free. Creating a new custom card with your own English, Traditional Chinese meaning and picture is an advanced feature.

Does every learner need another purchase?

No. Profiles within the app share the purchase, with unlimited additional learners and separate records and settings. This does not promise Apple Family Sharing.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase and connect to the internet. Open Settings → 解鎖進階學習, complete the parental calculation and tap 恢復購買 (Restore Purchases). Pending transactions must finish first. Cancellation or failure keeps free features available.

Will records appear automatically on a new device?

Restore Purchases restores the entitlement, not learning records, packs or worksheets. These are local. Device backup and restoration depend on iOS settings. Consider your backups before removing the app.

How does worksheet dictation work?

After unlocking, open 溫默書, choose Vocab or Paragraph, capture or import a printed English page, highlight and check the text. Vocab practises words or phrases; Paragraph practises sentences. Listen, type and review mistakes. Correct the recognition before starting.

What if the camera or recognition fails?

Import a photo instead. Use a clear, evenly lit, correctly oriented printed English page. Keep the image and retry or enter text manually. Change camera permission in iOS Settings. Handwriting and Chinese dictation are outside the main scope of this first release.

How do I change the voice or slow reading?

Open Settings → 英文朗讀 (English speech), choose voice, normal speed and pitch, preview and save. Each learner has separate preferences. Slow reading uses the same voice and pitch at a lower utterance rate. Availability depends on iOS.

Can I use it offline?

Built-in cards and games work offline. Recognition runs on the device. Speech and translation need available voice or language data; initial downloads need a connection. Purchasing and restoring need internet access. Translation failure does not block English dictation.

How can I remove local data?

Delete worksheets, packs and custom cards within the app. Historical dictation snapshots and one previous valid recovery copy may retain related text or images. To clear app data, use iOS Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage → this app → Delete App. Offload App keeps data. Manage system backups separately.
