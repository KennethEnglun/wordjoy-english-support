Privacy policy

How learning content, worksheets and purchase information are handled.

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the iOS/iPadOS app 字學貓英語樂園 (WordJoy English Paradise) and this support website, maintained by GitHub account KennethEnglun. It does not cover a separate Chinese-learning app with a similar name.

For technical or privacy questions, use the GitHub issue link on this site. It is public: describe general issues without children's information or other sensitive content.

2. Data stored on the device

The app stores learner nicknames, levels and preferences, game answers and history, mistake revision, stars, coins, stickers, packs and custom vocabulary locally. Selected custom-card photos, worksheet images, recognised text, highlights, corrections and dictation attempts are also local.

This supports practice, reports, speech and revision. Worksheets and packs are shared within the app; records are organised by learner. Profiles are not separate login accounts or confidential spaces.

There is no developer account system or developer-operated cloud database. The app does not automatically upload this learning content to us or synchronise it between devices.

3. Camera, photos, recognition and speech

Camera access is requested when you use capture. The system photo picker processes images you actively select. You may refuse camera access and import instead, or revoke permission in iOS Settings.

Recognition and English speech use Apple frameworks. Recognition runs on the device. Optional Chinese suggestions use iOS Translation, which may first download language data and then translate on the device. The app does not send worksheet or translation content to a developer server or third-party AI service.

The app has no recording or microphone-answer feature. System voices, language downloads and Apple services operate under Apple's settings and privacy policy.

4. Apple purchases and entitlement

Apple App Store and StoreKit handle purchases, confirmation and restoration. The app uses Apple-verified product and transaction information to determine access and handle pending, cancelled or revoked transactions.

We do not receive your card number, Apple Account password or payment verification codes. Apple handles account, payment and transaction data under its policies. Restoring entitlement does not send local learning content to the developer.

5. Advertising, analytics and children

This version has no third-party ads, advertising-identifier tracking or developer-integrated analytics/telemetry SDK. We do not sell learning data, build advertising profiles or use local learning content to train developer AI models.

Parents can use nicknames and avoid unnecessary personal information in worksheets or photos. The parental calculation restricts purchase and external-link entry; it is not consent to collect children's personal data.

6. Retention, deletion and backups

Local data normally remains until you remove the content or delete the app. Storage can retain one previous valid copy to recover from interrupted or corrupt writes. Referenced images may remain until a later save rotates that copy.

Editing or deleting a worksheet does not rewrite historical attempt snapshots, which may retain text and images. Deleting a pack does not automatically delete its custom vocabulary.

Use iOS Delete App to clear app data on a device. Offload App keeps documents and data. System backups or transfers may include app data, depending on iOS, iCloud or computer settings; manage those copies separately. Deleting the app does not delete Apple's purchase records.

We have no copy of your local content and cannot view, export or delete it from a server for you. Edit content in the app, withdraw system permissions or delete device data as described above.

7. Website and GitHub support

GitHub Pages serves this static website. We add no cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, external fonts or data-collection forms. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser information and request logs under its policy.

If you submit an issue, GitHub handles your account and submitted content; the maintainer uses it for support. This is separate from automatic app data collection. Edit your own GitHub content or ask us to remove content under our control; GitHub's retention and deletion rules apply to its service.

8. Policy updates

If data handling changes, we will update this page and date, and provide app information when needed. This policy describes features and data handling for the version represented by the date above.

Contact the developer

GitHub issues are public; submitting requires a GitHub account. Do not include children's names, photos, school worksheets, receipts, passwords or verification codes.
